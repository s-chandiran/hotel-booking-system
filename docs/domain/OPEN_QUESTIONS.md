# Open questions for the customer

Raised by the Gate 1 domain discovery ([data-model.md](data-model.md)) and revised after the Gate 1
review (2026-11-09). Each needs a confirmed answer before the dependent design is final. Until then,
the working assumption in the "If unanswered" column is used and marked ASSUMED in the domain doc.
**Decided** means the user has confirmed the scope for Release 1, and only the listed sub-points
remain open.

| # | Area | Question | Blocks | If unanswered (working assumption) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Property | **Decided for Release 1: single property.** Every property-owned record (rooms, room types, bookings, guests, invoices, payments, staff, settings, tax configuration, number sequences) carries a `property_id`, so multi-property can be added later without re-keying data. Release 1 has **no multi-tenant administration**: no property switcher, no cross-property users, no property onboarding. Still open: (a) Is a later chain or multi-property release planned? (b) What country is the property in (it affects tax and guest-registration rules)? (c) Can user accounts later span properties? | Gate 2 (tenant key on every table) | One property, India. `property_id` is present but always the same value. User accounts belong to one property |
| Q2 | Booking | Can one booking hold **several rooms** (family or group booking), or exactly one? | Gate 2 (booking ↔ room) | Exactly one room per booking |
| Q3 | Booking | **Decided for the demo (D-F, 2026-11-09): the room is assigned only at check-in.** Is the room **assigned at booking time or at check-in**? Is overbooking a room type ever allowed? | Gate 2, later mutations | A room type is chosen at booking, the room can be assigned up to check-in, and there is no overbooking |
| Q4 | Booking | Must guests on a booking be ≤ room capacity? Are extra beds or children counted differently? | Later mutations | Hard limit at capacity |
| Q5 | Pricing | Is the nightly rate per **room type** or per **room**? Are there seasonal or weekend rates, discounts, or extras (breakfast, minibar)? | Gate 2 (rate storage, invoice lines) | Rate per room type. No seasonal rates. The invoice is room charges only |
| Q6 | Tax | **Decided: tax is configuration, not a constant.** The 12% seen in `settingsMock.taxRate` is sample data and must not be hardcoded anywhere. Tax is held as one or more **tax components** (e.g. CGST and SGST, or a single GST line), each with a rate, an effective-from date and an order of application. When an invoice is issued, the components that applied are **copied onto the invoice as a tax snapshot** (component name, rate, taxable base, amount). Later configuration changes never alter an issued invoice. Still open: (a) Which components apply to this property, and do rates vary by tariff slab (room rate per night)? (b) Are prices tax-exclusive or tax-inclusive? (c) What rounding rule applies (per line, per component or per invoice) and to how many decimals? (d) Is tax charged on penalties and extras the same way as on room nights? | Gate 2 (tax configuration, invoice tax lines) | One component configured from settings, tax-exclusive pricing, rounded to 2 decimals per component per invoice, with the snapshot always stored |
| Q7 | Invoice | One invoice per booking, or can a stay be split (company and guest, interim bills, a separate invoice for a cancellation penalty)? Is the invoice issued at check-in, during the stay, or at check-out? See Q23 for payments received before the invoice exists. | Gate 2 | One final invoice per booking, issued at check-out, plus a separate invoice when a cancellation penalty is charged (Q13) |
| Q8 | Payments | How are **refunds** recorded? **Decided direction:** a refund is a **separate refund record** linked to the original payment. The original payment record is never modified or deleted. **Several partial refunds** against one payment are allowed, as long as total refunds ≤ the original amount. Still open: (a) Who can approve a refund, and is there a threshold that needs a second approver? (b) Must a refund go back by the original method (card to card), or can cash refunds be given against card payments? (c) Is an overpayment rejected, kept as guest credit, or refunded straight away? (d) Can a refund be reversed if it fails at the bank? | Gate 2 (payment and refund tables) | Refund rows with their own reference, amount, date, method, reason and status. Admin+ only. Same method as the original. Overpayment rejected |
| Q9 | Channels | Are Booking.com, Expedia and similar real integrations (channel manager), or just labels typed by staff? | Scope | Labels only, from a fixed list |
| Q10 | Booking | **Decided (D-B, 2026-11-09): staff explicitly confirm; walk-ins are created confirmed; a payment never confirms.** When is a booking `pending` vs `confirmed`: waiting for an **advance payment**, or waiting for staff approval? Can walk-ins go straight to `confirmed` or `checked_in`? If an advance payment confirms a booking, that payment is received **before any invoice exists**: see Q23 for how it is recorded. | Later mutations | `pending` until an advance payment or staff confirmation. Walk-ins are created as `confirmed` |
| Q11 | Numbering | **Decided for bookings and payments (D-E, 2026-11-09): bookings continue `BK-` after BK-10430; payments get a server-generated `PAY-` reference; both concurrency-safe. Invoice sub-points (a)–(e) stay open.** What formats do booking references, invoice numbers and payment references use? **Two separate requirements must be kept apart:** (1) **Uniqueness:** no two invoices share a number. A unique constraint guarantees this. (2) **Gap-free sequence:** every number in the series is used, with none skipped, which tax law or auditors may require. `AUTO_INCREMENT` and similar identity columns **do not** give a gap-free sequence: values are lost on rolled-back transactions, failed inserts and server restarts, and can be reserved in blocks. A gap-free series needs its own per-property counter, incremented inside the same transaction that issues the invoice, with the invoice's internal id kept separate from its legal number. Still open: (a) Is a gap-free invoice series legally required for this property? (b) Does the series reset each financial year (e.g. `INV/2026-27/0001`)? (c) Do receipts and refunds need their own legal series? (d) A voided invoice keeps its number, marked void and never reused (data-model.md F2.4). Is that what your auditor expects? (e) Is a correction after voiding a new invoice, or must a **credit note** be issued, with its own series? **Concurrency (decided requirement, data-model.md F2):** two users issuing at the same moment must never get the same number. The sequence row is locked inside the issuing transaction, and a unique key acts as the backstop. | Gate 2 (number sequences) | Invoices: a gap-free per-property, per-financial-year series, assigned only when issued. Voided numbers stay and are never reused. A correction is a new invoice. Bookings and payments: unique only, gaps allowed |
| Q12 | Checkout | **Decided (D-D, 2026-11-09): checkout is allowed with a balance, after a clear warning; the balance due stays correct.** Can a guest check out with a balance due? Is the front-desk "Balance due" the invoice amount minus payments received? With advance payments (Q23), is the balance before invoicing the booking total minus advances? | Later mutations | Balance = amount due − net payments (received − refunded). Checkout allowed, with a warning |
| Q13 | Cancellation | What happens on **cancellation** and on a **no-show**? These policies are **unresolved**, and each scenario needs an answer: (a) **Unpaid** booking cancelled: just cancelled, with no financial record? (b) **Partly paid** booking (advance received) cancelled: is the advance refunded in full, partly kept as a penalty, or kept as credit? (c) **Fully paid** booking cancelled: same question, and is a penalty invoice issued for the amount kept? (d) What is the **penalty policy**: free cancellation window, a percentage or first night's charge, different rules per channel or rate? (e) Is tax charged on a penalty (Q6d)? (f) Is a **no-show** its own status, and does it trigger a penalty automatically? (g) Who can waive a penalty? | Gate 2 (statuses, penalty invoice, refunds) | Unpaid → cancelled with no financial record. Paid or partly paid → the money is kept as an unallocated advance until a person decides on a refund or penalty. No automatic penalty. No-show isn't modelled in Release 1 |
| Q14 | Staff | How do staff job titles (Receptionist, Manager, Housekeeping, Accounts) map to login roles (front_desk, admin, super_admin)? Does every staff member get a login? **Who updates housekeeping status?** No login role exists for housekeeping staff today, so in Release 1 housekeeping changes would be made by `front_desk` (and above) on the housekeepers' behalf. Is that acceptable, or is a separate `housekeeping` role needed, limited to housekeeping status only? See the initial permission boundaries in data-model.md §10. | Gate 2 (user ↔ staff link), Gate 3 (`requireRole`) | A staff member has 0 or 1 login. Receptionist → front_desk, Manager/Accounts → admin. Housekeeping staff have no login, so front_desk+ updates housekeeping status |
| Q15 | Permissions | Which screens and figures can each role see? Please confirm or correct the initial boundaries in data-model.md §10. | Gate 3 (`requireRole`) | The initial boundaries in data-model.md §10 |
| Q16 | Revenue | **Decided for the admin dashboard (D-G, 2026-11-09): invoiced net (no tax) of issued invoices; void invoices and unallocated advances excluded. Whether front desk sees revenue stays as §10 (no).** Does "revenue" mean **payments received** or **invoices issued**? Room charges only, or with tax? Net of refunds? Should front desk staff see revenue figures? **Decided requirement (data-model.md F3):** unallocated advances are liabilities and never count as revenue, whichever basis is chosen. Still open: is revenue recognised when the invoice is issued, or per night stayed (relevant for stays that cross a month end in the monthly report)? | Gate 3 (dashboard and reports queries) | Invoiced room revenue excluding tax, net of void invoices, recognised on the issue date. Advances held are reported separately. Hidden (`null`) for front_desk |
| Q17 | Occupancy | Do rooms in maintenance count in the occupancy denominator? | Gate 3 | Excluded (sellable rooms only) |
| Q18 | Front desk | Is an arrival "time" the guest's expected arrival time captured on the booking, or just the property check-in time? | Gate 2 | An optional expected arrival time on the booking, defaulting to the property check-in time |
| Q19 | Privacy | **Required before a production release, not optional:** (a) How long are guest records (name, email, phone, stay history) kept, and what happens at the end of that period (delete, anonymise)? (c) Which data-protection law applies (e.g. India's DPDP Act 2023), and what consent, access and erasure requests must be supported? (d) Should PII be masked for some roles or in exports? (e) How long must financial records (invoices, payments, refunds) be kept, since this may be longer than guest PII? | Gate 2 (guest table, retention fields); **blocks production release** | Release 1 (demo) captures no ID documents and masks nothing, with retention TBD. **Production must not go live until (a)–(e) are answered** |
| Q20 | Check-in | Is early check-in (before the booked date or before the property check-in time) allowed? | Later mutations | Allowed on the arrival date only |
| Q21 | Maintenance | What happens to future bookings when a room is put into maintenance? | Later mutations | Block it if there are assigned future bookings |
| Q22 | Reports | What report filters are needed (date range, room type), and what export formats (CSV, PDF)? | Gate 3 (reports API) | Last 6 months fixed. No export in the first release |
| Q23 | Payments | **Can payments be received before an invoice is issued** (an advance or deposit at booking, a security deposit at check-in)? If so, **how are payments allocated to invoices**? (b) Can one payment be split across several invoices, e.g. room invoice and penalty invoice (Q7, Q13)? (c) Can one invoice be settled by several payments (OBSERVED: the `partial` status implies yes)? (d) Is a receipt document issued for an advance? (e) Is a security deposit a payment at all, or a separate hold that is released? (f) What happens to an unallocated advance when a booking is cancelled (Q13)? This question links Q7, Q10, Q12 and Q13. | Gate 2 (payment ↔ invoice relationship) | A payment always belongs to a booking and can be received before any invoice. An unallocated amount stays as the booking's advance. Security deposits aren't modelled in Release 1. The allocation and refund limits are fixed requirements (data-model.md F1) |
| Q24 | Tax | Is **tax due when an advance is received**, before the invoice is issued? The design must leave room to add receipt vouchers with tax lines later (data-model.md F3.5) |
